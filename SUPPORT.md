### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

**Q: How does multiplayer work?**
A: One player creates a room to generate a 4-letter room code (e.g. `ABCD`). Other players enter this code in the Lobby on their iPhone or iPad to join the room. All scores sync in real time.

**Q: What are the rules of Johann's Folly?**
A: Players start at 20 and work their way down the board to the Bullseye. Each turn gives you 3 darts to throw at your target. You must hit all 3 darts (no misses) to conquer the number and advance.

**Q: How do I change my name or reset data?**
A: Use **Change name** in the lobby. **Clear Cache & Start Fresh** resets saved rooms and settings and keeps your leaderboard history.
