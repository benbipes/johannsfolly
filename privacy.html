Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Welcome to Johann's Folly ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Johann's Folly mobile application and related multiplayer services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide core gameplay and multiplayer functionality:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register or sign in (via Email, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google), we store your player display name and email address.
  • Game & Scoring Data: Scores, dart statistics (such as Marks Per Round), room session participation, and career leaderboard statistics.
  • Device & Multiplayer Identifiers: Randomly generated temporary session keys used to synchronize multiplayer rooms across devices in real time.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide dart scoring, multiplayer room matchmaking, and live standings.
  • Maintain player leaderboards and game history.
  • Authenticate your account across sessions.
No Third-Party Tracking or Ads: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or analytics trackers.

3. Third-Party Authentication

If you sign in using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, authentication is handled securely through their respective identity platforms. We only receive your authorized display name and email address to create or link your game profile.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You have full control over your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, display name, registered email, and associated local game data directly within the app at any time by navigating to Lobby → Account Settings → Delete Account & Data.
  • Immediate Purge: Deleting your account immediately removes your active credentials and identity from the game.

5. Children's Privacy

Johann's Folly does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

All network communication for multiplayer room sync and account authentication is transmitted over industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS and TLS/WSS).
