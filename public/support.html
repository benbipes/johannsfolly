Frequently Asked Questions

How does multiplayer room sync work?
One player creates a room to generate a 4-letter room code. Other players enter this code in the Lobby to join and synchronize scoring in real time.

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Lobby → Account Settings → Delete Account & Data.
